ALPR siting law re-review, 2026-10-16 (delta from 2026-07-18 survey)

Result: no new state siting law beyond WA and VA. No rule entries added.

Method: web research (secondary trackers plus primary text where readable).
Secondary: recordinglaw.com 50-state comparison (as of 2026-10-01, 26 states
with ALPR-specific statutes), multistate.us (2026-09-23), LAPPA, news.

New 2026 enactments checked
- NM SB 40 (eff 2026-07-01): sharing limits, reporting. Text read. No siting.
- KY HB 58 (eff 2026-07-15): use/retention/disclosure. Secondary sources only;
  rights-of-way permit via Transportation Cabinet (permitting, not protected
  locations). Confirm against statute text next review.
- CT PA 26-14 sec. 13 (signed 2026-05-04; operating limits eff 2026-10-01):
  use, 21-day retention, sharing. BORDERLINE: fixed readers barred on the
  premises of, or within a POST Council-set distance of, registered
  gender-affirming/reproductive health facilities and immigrant-serving
  nonprofits. Secondary source (recordinglaw.com); statute PDF not readable.
  Not modeled: opt-in registry, no numeric distance yet, categories differ
  from tracked ones. Watch for the Council model policy (due 2026-12-01).
- WA SB 6002, VA HB 2724: unchanged.

Executive actions (not statutes, not protected-location siting)
- FL: FDOT EOM 26-01 (2026-08-31) revoked ALPR right-of-way permits on state
  highways.
- TX: TxDOT paused new ALPR permits on state roads (Sept 2026) at the
  Governor's direction; existing cameras remain.
- MO: executive order on ALPR safeguards (use rules).

Pending / next session: IN, NV, OH, UT, VA (expanded), AL, CA, CO.
Vermont: state-agency ALPR use ban scheduled 2027-07-01 (use ban, not siting).
