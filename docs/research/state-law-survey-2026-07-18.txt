## Research Summary

After consulting the LAPPA September 2025 50-state PDF summary, Stateline's October 2025 article, unflocked.org, obscureiq.com, recordinglaw.com, and directly reading primary statute texts for Idaho (§ 49-1432), Montana (MCA § 46-5-117), Vermont (23 V.S.A. § 1607), Florida (§ 316.0777), Tennessee (§ 55-10-302), Minnesota (§ 13.824), and Illinois (625 ILCS 5/2-130), I find:

**No state among the 43 remaining has a statute specifically restricting ALPR siting/placement near schools, worship, healthcare, courthouses, food banks, or other protected-location categories.** Washington (SB 6002, already handled) is, as of July 2026, the only state with such a siting law. 13 of the 43 states have some ALPR statute addressing use, retention, or data sharing. 31 states/DC have no ALPR-specific statute at all (including several with pending bills). Several new 2026 laws (Connecticut PA 26-14, New Jersey A4070) are recent but focus on data use/sharing, not siting.

## Key Findings and Caveats

### Siting-Law Finding
**Washington (SB 6002, 2026) remains the sole state with a statute restricting ALPR placement/data-capture near protected location categories.** No other state in this survey has a siting restriction even close to that model. Several states have DOT right-of-way permit requirements (Florida § 316.0777, Georgia DOT administrative permit, Utah UDOT special-use permit, CDOT checklist for Colorado) - but these are infrastructure/access permits comparable to requiring any entity to get permission to install hardware in a road ROW, not proximity bans near sensitive institutions.

### 2025–2026 Legislation (Specifically Checked)
- **Arkansas**: Act 668/SB 446 (2025) ✅ enacted - use/retention, no siting
- **Colorado**: SB 26-070 (2026) ❌ died Senate floor April 29, 2026 - no ALPR statute
- **Connecticut**: PA 26-14 (2026) ✅ enacted, eff. Oct 1, 2026 - retention/use, no siting *(NEW - note for your rules file)*
- **Idaho**: S1180 (2025) ✅ enacted July 1, 2025 - use/access, no siting
- **Illinois**: PA 103-540 (2024) ✅ enacted - data sharing, no siting
- **New Jersey**: A4070 (2026) ✅ enacted March 25, 2026 - data sharing, no siting *(NEW)*
- **Montana**: SB 344 (2023) ✅ enacted - near-prohibition with LE exceptions, no siting
- **Tennessee**: § 55-10-302 (2014) ✅ existing statute - 90-day retention, no siting
- **Texas**: HB 2083 (2025) ❌ died in committee - no statute
- **Virginia**: HB 2724 (2025) - *(already handled by user)*

### Confidence Flags
- **Montana SB 344 (2023)**: The unflocked.org characterization as "warrant required" is inaccurate; actual MCA § 46-5-117 text requires specific enumerated LE purposes - stricter than most states but not technically a warrant.
- **Alabama**: Has administrative rules (Ala. Admin. Code r. 265-X-6) but no enacted statute from the legislature; the admin code is authorized by Code of Ala. § 41-9-620 (ACJIC authority), which is not an ALPR-specific statute.
- **Colorado § 24-72-113**: Applies to ALPR imagery as "passive surveillance records" per CDOT guidance, but is not an ALPR-specific statute - do not classify as `no_siting_law`.
- **South Carolina H.4013**: Pending only; SC has **no enacted ALPR statute**; note the 2025 state court ruling affirming SLED authority.
- **New York**: Bills have passed the Assembly since 2015 but consistently died in the Senate; the pattern is strong enough to classify as `no_alpr_statute` with high confidence.
- **Connecticut**: Prior reference to § 14-59a as an ALPR statute appears to be an error in some secondary sources - the actual ALPR law is PA 26-14 (2026).
