About this tool

New here? The How & why page explains everything in plain English.

Foul Flock cross-references crowdsourced ALPR camera locations (OpenStreetMap / DeFlock tagging) against a hand-curated table of state camera-siting statutes, and flags cameras within a screened distance of a protected place: schools, places of worship, healthcare facilities, courts, food banks.

⚠️ Read before acting on anything here

  • This is not legal advice. It's a place to start research, not to end it.
  • The data can be wrong. Camera positions are crowdsourced: cameras move, get removed, get mis-tagged. Place data has the same problem.
  • Some distances are guesses. When a law says "immediately surrounding" instead of giving a number, this map substitutes an adjustable radius and labels it. That number is ours, not the law's.
  • Statutes change. Check the citation and the effective date, then read the current text yourself.
  • Verify before you file. In person, or through imagery and public records, before contacting any agency.

How to act on a flag

  1. Click a flagged camera and review the statute citation and distance.
  2. Confirm the camera and the protected place actually exist where mapped.
  3. Read the statute at the linked source. Confirm it's in effect and applies.
  4. Use "Draft report" for a starting-point letter (records request / relocation request), then replace its contents with facts you've verified yourself.
